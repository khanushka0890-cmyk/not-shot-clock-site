Fast Pool Rules

Speed variants of 8-ball and 9-ball, built around an 8-second shot clock. Based on International rules, with a few changes for speed play.

The rack

  • 9 object balls: 4 solids, 4 stripes, and the black.
  • Racked as a low rhombus, not the standard triangle.

The shot clock

  • 8 seconds per shot.
  • Starts the moment all balls from the previous shot come to rest; stops the instant the cue tip strikes the cue ball.
  • Using a rest? Add a 3-second extension.
  • Go over the limit and it's ball-in-hand to your opponent.

Ball-in-hand

  • Can be placed anywhere on the table, including right against a pocket.
  • The shot clock freezes the moment it becomes ball-in-hand, and starts again the instant the incoming player touches the cue ball.

Break scratch

  • If the breaker scratches, the opponent gets ball-in-hand behind the line.
  • They may only shoot at balls outside the kitchen (shaded), and may only pot into the four pockets outside the kitchen end.
