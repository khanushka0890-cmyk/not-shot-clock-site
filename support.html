Common questions

How do I change the time per shot?

On the Settings screen (the first screen you see when the app opens), use the +/− buttons or tap one of the preset pills (5s, 8s, 10s, 15s, 24s). Your choice is remembered next time you open the app.

Can I change the time mid-session?

Yes — tap "‹ Back" in the top-left of the timer screen to go back and adjust it, then start again.

Can I show the clock on a TV?

Yes, two ways: tap the Cast icon on the timer screen to send it to a Chromecast, or use iOS Screen Mirroring (swipe down from the top-right corner → Screen Mirroring) to any AirPlay-compatible TV or Apple TV.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. The app doesn't use accounts, analytics, or trackers, and nothing is sent off your device. See the full privacy policy for details.
