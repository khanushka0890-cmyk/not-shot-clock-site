Common questions

How do I change the time per shot?

On the main screen, spin the dial to the number of seconds you want, or tap the number in the middle and type it. Your choice is remembered next time you open the app.

Can I change the time mid-game?

Yes — tap "‹ Back" in the top-left of the timer screen to go back and adjust it, then tap Start again.

How does extension work?

On the main screen, tap Add Extension and enter how many extra seconds a shot can get. During a shot, tap the amber Extension button to give that shot the extra time; tap it again to undo. Tap the extension button on the main screen again to turn it off.

Can I show the clock on a TV?

Yes, with AirPlay. First make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. Then swipe down from the top-right corner of your iPhone to open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and pick your Apple TV or AirPlay TV. The TV switches to a full-screen clock with the last five shots automatically, and your phone keeps the controls. You can also tap the TV button in the top-right corner of the app for these steps. Chromecast is not supported.

How does the free trial work?

The first time you tap Start, you can start a free 30-day trial with full access. Nothing is charged automatically. When the trial ends, you can unlock the app forever with a one-time payment of $2.99 — no subscription.

I already paid — how do I get my unlock back on a new phone?

Open the unlock screen (tap Unlock under the Start button) and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. The app doesn't use accounts, analytics, or trackers, and nothing is sent off your device. See the full privacy policy for details.
