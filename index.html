Pocket Pool App

Not Shot Clock

A shot clock built for pool nights — tap to start, glance at the ring, hear the buzzer.

Not Shot Clock is a fast, no-nonsense shot clock built for fast pool. Fast pool needs a real shot clock — something everyone at the table can glance at, that buzzes when time's up, and doesn't get in the way of the game. That's it. That's the app.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Set your time per shot — presets for 5s, 8s, 10s, 15s, 24s, or dial in your own
  • A big, glanceable ring and readout that's easy to see mid-game
  • Audible cues for start, the final seconds, and time's up — no need to stare at the screen
  • Tap once to acknowledge time's up and log the shot, tap again to start the next one
  • Cast the timer to a TV via Chromecast, or mirror your screen via AirPlay
