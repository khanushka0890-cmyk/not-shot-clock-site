How it works

Three taps, no arguments.

Set the time

Spin the rotary dial, or tap the number and type it. Any time per shot, no fixed presets.

Run the clock

Tap to start. Green runs, amber warns at three seconds, red means the buzzer's going off.

Add extension

Turn extension on once. During any shot, one tap gives it extra seconds — tap again to undo.

The dial

A dial that means business.

Green while you line up the shot. Amber the moment three seconds are left, with a beep to match. Red is a loud, sharp buzzer and a glow you can't miss from across the table.

  • A distinct sound and haptic for every stage
  • Big numbers you can read from across the room
  • Counts up past zero, so you know exactly how far over
Set it your way

Need more time? One tap.

Tap Add Extension on the main screen and pick how many seconds. Then, during any shot, the amber Extension button gives that shot extra time — and if it was a mis-tap, tap again to undo. The buzzer only fires when the extra time runs out too.

  • Any number of extra seconds
  • Tap to extend, tap again to undo
  • Every shot is tagged DONE, EXT or OVER in the log
AirPlay

Put it on the TV.

Mirror your iPhone to an Apple TV or any AirPlay TV and the app switches the screen to a layout built for the room: a big dial, the last five shots, and an Extended badge when a shot gets extra time. Your phone keeps the controls.

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
  2. Tap Screen Mirroring and pick your TV.
  3. The TV switches to the full-screen clock automatically.
  • Runs off the same clock as your phone — no lag
  • Sound plays through the TV
Shot history

Know your night.

Every shot is saved the moment it's over. Games are grouped into nights automatically, with your fastest, slowest and average shot for each game and each night.

  • Your all-time fastest shot gets a star
  • Nothing to start or end — just play
  • Saved on your phone, private to you
