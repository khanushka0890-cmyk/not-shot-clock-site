Not Shot Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version: Not Shot Clock doesn't collect, store, or send any personal data anywhere. It's a countdown timer that runs entirely on your device. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.

Data collection

Not Shot Clock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services
  • Does not create user accounts and does not require sign-in
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, or microphone
  • Does not connect to any server we operate, and does not transmit any data off your device

Camera

The app includes an optional, in-development Auto-Detect feature that can use the camera to watch the table and automatically start or stop the shot clock. This feature is not yet enabled in the app's visible settings. If and when it is enabled, camera data is processed entirely on your device to detect motion — it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Showing the clock on a TV

When you mirror your iPhone to a TV with AirPlay, the app shows its own TV layout on that screen. This is handled entirely by iOS and your TV on your own network; no data is sent to us.

Free trial and purchase

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes any payment; we never see or receive your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that the trial has started or the unlock was purchased, which it uses to decide whether the timer is available.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings (time per shot and extension), your shot history, and the date your free trial started, locally on your device only, using standard iOS local storage. None of this leaves your device or is sent anywhere. It's deleted automatically if you delete the app (your trial and purchase status are kept by Apple, so they can be restored).

Third-party services

The app loads typefaces (Big Shoulders, Sora and Inter) from Google Fonts over the internet when it opens, purely for display purposes. As with any web request, Google may log standard technical information such as IP address as part of serving that request; this is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not send Google, or any other third party, any information about you.

This website

This website (notshotclock.pocketpoolapp.com) counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics so we can see how many people visit and which pages they read. It uses no cookies, doesn't fingerprint or follow you across sites, and collects no personal data. The app itself still has no analytics.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support contact on the support page.
